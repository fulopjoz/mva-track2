## Q1. The ~6% and ~13% BUBR1 abundance values

### Experimental system — inducible shRNA titration in HeLa: CONFIRMED-VERBATIM

Verbatim (Results & Discussion, section "MVA-associated mutations in the kinase domain of BUBR1 cause chromosome segregation defects primarily by lowering protein abundance", third line of evidence; p. 6 of the author-manuscript PDF):

> "Third, the amount of segregation errors is highly sensitive to the amount of BUBR1 present in the cell. BUBR1 abundance was reduced to different levels by **titration of doxycyline to a HeLa cell line stably carrying inducible expression of an shRNA to BUBR1**. Whereas saturating amounts of doxycyline reduced **BUBR1 protein to ~6%** and caused chromosome mis-segregation in the majority of cells, low amounts of doxycyline, while still significantly reducing **BUBR1 levels to ~13%**, had virtually no impact on the fidelity of chromosome segregation (Fig. 4D, lower graph)."

Verbatim (Fig. 4 caption, panel D lower):

> "(D, lower) Analysis of chromosome segregation by live imaging of **HeLa cells expressing inducible BUBR1 shRNA and H2B-EYFP treated with 0, 1 or 1000 ng/ml doxycycline**. Graph indicates the percentage of cells with chromosome mis-segregations (average of 3 experiments, a total of at least 350 cells, ± s.e.m.). Quantitative immunoblot shows the expression of BUBR1 and Tubulin in lysates from a representative experiment, band intensity of BUBR1/Tubulin relative to untreated control is indicated (average of 3 experiments)"

- **System:** YES — a HeLa line with doxycycline-inducible shRNA against BUBR1; abundance titrated by doxycycline dose. Doses tested: 0, 1 and 1000 ng/ml; "saturating" = 1000 ng/ml → ~6%; "low" = 1 ng/ml → ~13% (only two non-zero doses appear in the panel, so the mapping is unambiguous). [CONFIRMED-VERBATIM for doses; dose↔value mapping from text + caption together]
- **Quantification basis of the percentages:** BUBR1/tubulin immunoblot band intensity relative to the untreated (0 doxycycline) control, average of 3 experiments. [CONFIRMED-VERBATIM, Fig. 4D-lower caption]
- The inducible line is consistent with "HTR34" described in Materials & Methods ("created by infection of HeLa cells stably expressing Tet repressor (a gift of M. Timmers) with retrovirus carrying pSuperior-retro-puro-BUBR1") [CONFIRMED-VERBATIM for the Methods sentence]; however, the Fig. 4D caption does not name the line, so equating the Fig 4D-lower cells with HTR34 is [INFERENCE], not a statement of the paper.

### Location — CONFIRMED-VERBATIM

- **Section:** Results & Discussion, under the heading "**MVA-associated mutations in the kinase domain of BUBR1 cause chromosome segregation defects primarily by lowering protein abundance**" (the passage is the third of "three lines of evidence" in that section).
- **Figure: Fig. 4D, lower graph** — confirmed. Fig. 4 is titled "Low BUBR1 levels is the primary cause of BUBR1 dysfunction by MVA Mutations in the kinase domain". Within panel D: upper graph = forced overexpression of I909T/L1012P to wild-type levels (nocodazole response, U2OS, flow cytometry); middle graph = L844F vs Q921H (chromosome mis-segregation, U2OS, live imaging); **lower graph = the doxycycline shRNA titration (HeLa, live imaging + immunoblot)**. The in-text citation "(Fig. 4D, lower graph)" is verbatim in both the PMC HTML and the Hubrecht-hosted PDF.

### The endpoint separating ~6% from ~13% — chromosome mis-segregation frequency, NOT aneuploidy onset

- The measured endpoint is "**the percentage of cells with chromosome mis-segregations**", scored by live-cell imaging of H2B-EYFP-expressing cells (average of 3 experiments, ≥350 cells). At ~6% BUBR1: "chromosome mis-segregation in the majority of cells". At ~13%: "virtually no impact on the fidelity of chromosome segregation". [CONFIRMED-VERBATIM]
- **It is NOT aneuploidy onset.** No karyotype, FISH, or aneuploid-cell-fraction measurement is part of this experiment (or anywhere in the paper); the panel scores acute mis-segregation events during mitosis in a cancer cell line.
- **It is also not, strictly, a checkpoint-failure readout.** Mitotic checkpoint arrest was assayed separately (nocodazole + MPM-2 flow cytometry; Fig. 4A and Fig. 4D-upper). The authors *interpret* the titration as supporting the abundance mechanism for checkpoint deficiency ("These findings support the hypothesis that mitotic checkpoint deficiency in MVA patient cells carrying mutations in the BUBR1 kinase domain is caused by low BUBR1 levels." [CONFIRMED-VERBATIM]), but the endpoint measured in Fig 4D-lower is segregation fidelity, not checkpoint arrest.

## Q2. What was measured in patient-derived biallelic BUB1B cells

### Patients and cell lines — CONFIRMED-VERBATIM (main text + Supplemental Table 1)

Verbatim (Results & Discussion, first section): "we established lymphoblastoid and fibroblast cell lines of MVA patients as well as of their respective parents or unrelated healthy individuals (an overview is presented in Supplementary Table 1)."

Four biallelic patients' lines were studied (genotypes as named by the paper; cell type per Supplemental Table 1, column "Patient cells?"):

| Genotype (allele 1 / allele 2) | Cell type | Note |
|---|---|---|
| absent/I909T | EBV-transformed lymphoblastoid | "absent" allele = "no transcript", mutation unknown |
| 386X/R727C | lymphoblastoid | 386X = 1156C>T (new in this paper); R727C new in this paper |
| 753X/R814H | SV40-transformed fibroblast | 753X = 2211-2insGTTA, S738fsX753 |
| 731X/Y155C | SV40-transformed fibroblast | 731X = IVS16-2A>G (new in this paper); Y155C new in this paper |

- Parental (heterozygous) control lines used in comparisons: **386X/WT** and **731X/WT**. [CONFIRMED-VERBATIM]
- Lymphoblastoid lines were obtained from ECACC or Coriell repositories; fibroblasts were primary lines subsequently SV40-transformed (by H. Hanenberg); "All samples were obtained with informed consent from the family and under multicenter ethics approval (MREC05/02/17)." [CONFIRMED-VERBATIM]
- Supplemental Table 1 ("Overview of MVA mutations in BUB1B", read in full) lists all alleles with nucleotide changes and predicted protein junctions: truncations 194X (580C>T), 386X (1156C>T*), 483X (IVS10-1G>T, Q467fsX483, ∆exon11), 731X (IVS16-2A>G*), 753X (2211-2insGTTA, S738fsX753), "Absent" (unknown, no transcript); substitutions L844F & Q921H (both in one allele), R727C*, R550Q, Y155C*, R814H, L1012P, I909T. Footnote: "* All mutations except those marked with * have been described (Hanks et al., 2004)" — i.e. 1156C>T (386X), IVS16-2A>G (731X), R727C and Y155C were newly identified in this paper. "Patient cells?" = No for L844F&Q921H, R550Q, L1012P (tested only by plasmid expression in U2OS/HeLa). [CONFIRMED-VERBATIM]
- Population size: 4 biallelic patients (+ parents + unrelated healthy controls). The mutation background is "Mono- and biallelic mutations in BUB1B were identified in British and Japanese MVA patients" (refs 2 and 3 therein = Hanks 2004 Nat Genet; Matsuura 2006); the paper does not state the origin of each individual patient. Cell numbers per assay are given below with each result.

### Assays and quantitative results in patient cells — CONFIRMED-VERBATIM

1. **Mitotic checkpoint, nocodazole 16 h (MPM-2 flow cytometry, 4N population; Fig 1A; ≥3 (lymphoblastoid) / ≥5 (fibroblast) experiments):** MVA patient cells "failed to accumulate in mitosis in checkpoint dependent manner after treatment with the spindle depolymerizing drug nocodazole". No numeric values quoted in the text (values are in the Fig 1A graphs; see UNVERIFIABLE item 2).
2. **Mitotic exit timing, STLC (DIC time-lapse, fibroblast lines; Fig 1B; 3 experiments, 300 cells total):** "Whereas control STLC treated cells were delayed in mitosis for the duration of the experiment, **50% of the population of MVA patient cell lines exited within 68 (753X/R814H) and 114 min (731X/Y155C)**."
3. **Chromosome alignment (MG132 90 min, fibroblast lines; Fig 1C; 4 experiments, ≥70 cells/experiment):** "MVA patient cells, treated with proteasome inhibitor MG132 to prevent mitotic exit, showed a **2-3 fold increase in the amount of chromosome misalignment** compared to control lines."
4. **BUBR1 protein abundance (quantitative immunoblot; Fig 2A; 4 experiments):** "abundance of full-length substitution mutant BUBR1 protein in all patient lines (absent/I909T, 386X/R727C, 753X/R814H or 731X/Y155C) was **decreased 2-6 fold compared to wild-type alleles**"; "levels of full-length BUBR1 in the parent cell lines, carrying one mutant allele, were consistently in between those of patients and unrelated healthy controls"; "None of the truncated proteins, 386X, 731X and 753X, could be detected in lysates of MVA patient cell lines or in parental controls."
5. **Kinetochore-localized BUBR1 (BUBR1/ACA immunofluorescence quantification; Fig 2B; 3 experiments, 25 kinetochores per cell, 5 cells per experiment):** "Low overall protein abundance translated to low levels of BUBR1 on unattached kinetochores"; with enhanced contrast "the residual protein could be recruited to kinetochores, indicative of at least partial functionality of mutant BUBR1".
6. **mRNA (Northern blot; Fig 3A):** "mRNA expression of three substitution alleles, I909T, R727C and Y155C, was unaffected"; "mRNA from the 386X allele could not be detected"; "the **2-fold reduction** in mRNA levels of 731X/WT relative to healthy controls suggested that transcripts from the 731X allele were also absent".
7. **Protein turnover (cycloheximide 5 h, lymphoblastoid lines; Fig 3B; 3 experiments):** "the protein turnover of the substitution mutant proteins I909T and R727C was **increased ~2-fold** compared to wild-type protein". (In U2OS: HSP90 inhibitor geldanamycin severely decreased mutant but not wild-type LAP-BUBR1; MG132 prevented the geldanamycin-induced turnover — mechanistic probes of misfolding/proteasomal clearance.)
8. **Rescue by ectopic wild-type BUBR1 (transient LAP-BUBR1 expression, fibroblast lines; Fig 1D; 5 experiments):** "restoration of high levels of BUBR1 by transient expression of Localization and Affinity Purification (LAP)-tagged BUB1B cDNA ... induced a **2-fold increase in the response to nocodazole** in patient cells"; and for 731X/Y155C vs its parental control 731X/WT, "mitotic checkpoint activity was **almost fully restored to parental levels** upon re-expression of BUBR1 in the patient cells".

**Not measured in patient cells:** no karyotype or aneuploid-fraction quantification; no BUBR1 level expressed as a percent of normal (only fold-changes of the full-length band); no drug treatment of patient cells; no dose-response of rescued BUBR1 in patient cells (the rescue is a single overexpression condition of wild-type LAP-BUBR1).

## Q3. Does the paper establish any patient-specific therapeutic threshold?

**NO.** Grounds, all checked against the primary text on 2026-10-19:

1. **The words "therape*" and "threshold" do not appear anywhere in the article** (full-text search of the PMC full text: 0 hits for each; "treatment" occurs only for in-vitro drug treatments of cells).
2. The ~6%/~13% comparison was performed in **HeLa carcinoma cells** in which endogenous (wild-type) BUBR1 was depleted by inducible shRNA; the endpoint is acute chromosome mis-segregation frequency in live-imaged mitoses — not aneuploidy, tumour risk, or any clinical outcome. Doxycycline is the shRNA inducer, not a therapeutic agent.
3. Patient BUBR1 abundance is reported only as a fold-reduction of the full-length (missense-mutant) band (2-6 fold, i.e. roughly 17–50% of the control full-length band) and is **never** placed on the 0–100% scale of the HeLa titration or linked to any phenotype threshold in patients.
4. The patient-cell rescue (Fig 1D) is a causality experiment with wild-type LAP-BUBR1 expressed at "high levels" — one condition, not a titration; the paper draws no inference about how much BUBR1 a patient would need.
5. No compound that raises BUBR1 is identified. Of the drugs used, the HSP90 inhibitor geldanamycin **decreases** mutant BUBR1 levels, and MG132 (proteasome inhibition) is used only as a mechanistic probe of mutant turnover in U2OS cells; neither is proposed as, or discussed as, therapy.
6. **Interpretive guard [ANALYSIS — this is my observation, not a claim of the paper]:** HeLa cells retain faithful segregation at ~13% wild-type BUBR1, whereas patient cells carrying full-length (mutant) BUBR1 reduced only 2–6 fold show severe checkpoint and alignment defects. These observations are not on a common scale (wild-type vs mutant protein; cancer line vs lymphoblasts/fibroblasts; different endpoints), and the paper never compares them. The ~6%/~13% figures therefore cannot be transferred as a patient therapeutic target level (e.g. "restore above ~13%") without assumptions the paper does not make. The paper's own conclusion is mechanistic only: "we propose that chromosomal instability in MVA patients carrying BUB1B mutations is a result of low BUBR1 protein abundance."

## Study-system summary (contract fields)

- **Organism/system:** human — MVA patient-derived EBV-lymphoblastoid and SV40-transformed fibroblast lines (+ parental and unrelated healthy controls); HeLa and U2OS cancer cell lines for shRNA-replacement and inducible-depletion experiments. No animal work.
- **Population size:** 4 biallelic patients' lines (+ parents + healthy controls); cell counts per assay as quoted above (e.g. ≥350 cells for the Fig 4D-lower titration; 300 cells for Fig 1B).
- **Intervention + control (Q1 experiment):** doxycycline-inducible BUBR1 shRNA in HeLa at 0 / 1 / 1000 ng/ml (untreated = 0 ng/ml is the reference level, 100%).
- **Endpoint measures:** chromosome mis-segregation frequency (live H2B-EYFP imaging); BUBR1/tubulin immunoblot band intensity; (elsewhere) MPM-2 mitotic index by flow cytometry, chromosome alignment after MG132, Northern blot, cycloheximide chase.
- **Direct vs indirect relevance to MVA (biallelic BUB1B):** DIRECT — a primary mechanistic study OF biallelic BUB1B MVA patient cells; the origin of the "low BUBR1 protein abundance" explanation for kinase-domain MVA mutants and of the observation that wild-type BUBR1 re-expression restores checkpoint activity in patient cells.
- **Contradictory evidence / internal nuances (within the same paper):** (i) Y155C is a separation-of-function allele — stable protein that restores alignment but not checkpoint — so the 731X/Y155C patient's defect is not purely abundance-mediated; the paper's model for such patients is "a reduction in protein combined with direct functional impairment". (ii) LAP-731X (lacking the kinase domain) restored both checkpoint and alignment in replacement assays, so the kinase domain is dispensable for those functions in these assays; kinase-domain MVA mutations act via stability. (iii) Forced overexpression of I909T and L1012P to wild-type levels fully restored the nocodazole response, so those substitutions do not directly cripple BUBR1 beyond lowering its abundance.
- **What this paper does NOT establish:** any therapeutic threshold or target BUBR1 level for patients; any aneuploidy-onset threshold (endpoint was mis-segregation frequency); BUBR1 as percent of normal in patients; any drug or intervention that raises BUBR1; applicability of the HeLa 6–13% window to patient cells, primary cells, or any in-vivo context.
