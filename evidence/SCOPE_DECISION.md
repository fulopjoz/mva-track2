1. **The named-candidate requirement lost its "mechanism" cover.** Tang 2011 mTORi neutrality
   + our AMPK reopen mean everolimus can no longer claim resting mechanism evidence; it must be
   framed as safety-precedent-driven with decision-tree gating. Scope ADD: the report needs an
   explicit "what we do NOT claim" section - exclusion jurisprudence (HSP90i contra; KIF18Ai;
   TRIP13i; direct SACi; autophagy-flux) is now a first-class deliverable, and is scarce among
   competing submissions.
2. **ASO skipping became the precision story but is conditional.** Every ASO sentence must carry
   the Δ716-761+E715G conditional (kimi F1/F2: allele non-specificity; KARD off-by-one). Scope
   ADD: assay-spec section (the six-assay stack) is load-bearing, not appendix-level.
3. **Framing needs a precedent census to survive.** "No approved preventive in constitutional
   chromosomal-instability syndromes" is scope-safe ONLY after wave-2 Q5 census (CAPP2 ambushed
   pass 1). Scope ADD: a complete prevention-in-predisposition table in the report.
4. **Honesty artifacts are scoring assets.** NMD-geometry exactness, allele math, regulatory
   collapse of ataluren, GenAI-provenance disclosure (hackathon methods rule) - required, not
   optional.
5. **Unchanged scope:** Track 1 diagnosis runs independently; Track 2's data dependence on Track
   1 is nil (report reads the proband genotype from the challenge doc, not our pipeline).
