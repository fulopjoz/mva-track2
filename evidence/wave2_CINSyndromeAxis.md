# Wave-2 Q4 - CIN / Constitutional-Cancer-Predisposition Precedent Axis

**Axis**: What is actually prescribed today in chromosomal-instability / constitutional-cancer-risk
children; which precedent class frames the Track-2 MVA (BUB1B p.Leu737Ter + p.Asn1002Lys) submission
strongest. Date: 2026-09-05. All PMIDs/DOIs PubMed-verified 2026-09-05; load-bearing claims scite-graded
(s/c/m = supporting/contrasting/mentioning). Human vs preclinical flagged per claim; outright
inferences tagged [INFERENCE].

---

## VERDICT (verdict-first)

> **No constitutional CIN or genomic-instability disorder currently has an approved pharmacologic
> agent for cancer *prevention*; management is surveillance-only** (AACR 2024 Childhood Cancer
> Predisposition Workshop). The strongest submission frame for MVA is the
> **everolimus–TSC analogy** (a CIN-class disorder with an approved, pediatric-legal, mechanism-based
> drug repurposed from transplant → syndrome care) combined with the **Li‑Fraumeni metformin "precision-prevention"**
> trial-model: borrow a decades-standing, widely-used, low-cost chronic drug; run a biomarker-gated
> precision-prevention trial alongside, not instead of, surveillance. Second strongest frame:
> **lonafarnib–HGPS** (a progeroid CIN/instability disorder where a true repurposed targeted drug gained
> FDA approval primarily on non-traditional survival endpoints in an ultrarare pediatric population  - 
> an accelerated-approval-on-surrogate-survival-signal model). The
> **interferonopathy JAKinib class (baricitinib/ruxolitinib)** is the template for how a
> pediatric-lethal-inflammation CIN-adjacent field mobilized off-label pathway pharmacology to practice
> 3+ yrs before any drug approval, via mechanistic ex vivo data → case series → cohort registries;
> it is the "rapid compassionate-access pathway" template. Key contrast-shock: **the one drug that WAS
> approved in a constitutional cancer-risk disorder for prevention - celecoxib in FAP (2000 RCT +
> 1999 approval) - was WITHDRAWN (Pfizer voluntary, 2012) on long-term cardiovascular safety**; no
> current constitutional-cancer-risk prevention drug exists. MVA submission therefore sits in the
> "surveillance-only with no approved prevention" category and can pursue the TSC/lonafarnib pathway
> only via sustained Durability/Mechanistic work, not via approval-year precedent.

Bottom line for the join: frame the MVA proposal as a **precision-prevention / mechanism-directed
repurposing axis** anchored to (i) everolimus-TSC as the CIN-class success template (approved drug,
pediatric, mechanism-matched), and (ii) the LFS-MILI metformin trial structure as the registry-linked,
biomarker-gated, "winning endpoint can be non-tumor" design. List as explicit failure modes any
reference to NSAID/COX "prevention" equivalence (celecoxib withdrawal) and any claim of an existing
approved preventive pharmacology (none exists).

This is the **only** constitutional tumor-risk disorder with an approved, mechanism-matched,
chronically-pediatric-safe pharmacology.

## Synthesis matrices

### Precedent-class ranking for the MVA framing (strongest first)

1) **Everolimus-TSC** (approved, mechanism-matched, pediatric, chronic-safe) - the "CIN disorder
   with approved pharmacology" closest analog. Use for: mechanism-matched-pathway framing,
   chronic-pediatric feasibility precedent, impairment-of-pathology-on-target framing,
   pediatric-legal approval existence.
2) **LFS-metformin precision-prevention** (MILI RCT design) - borrow a longstanding, safe,
   pediatric-legal drug; run a **biomarker-gated precision-prevention trial** alongside
   surveillance; tumor-free survival is the winning endpoint; explicitly the "fundable,
   ethics-defensible design for unmet childhood syndromes.
3) **Lonafarnib-HGPS** - accelerated ultrarare pediatric CIN approval on non-traditional
   efficacy endpoints (mortality surrogate); a "pathway to approval for an extremely rare
   pediatric chronic condition.
4) **Interferonopathy JAKinibs** - proves mechanism→compassionate-use→practice adoption in
   ~5 yrs without approval; a "what's feasibly achievable in the near-term" template.
5) **Celecoxib-FAP** - TWO-SIDED: only proof that prevention labeling is regulatory-feasible in
   this class; also a CV-safety reversal; cite as the "existence proof" AND "stability warning"

### What the MVA submission should and should not claim

- **Should claim**: (a) Management is surveillance-only in 2024/25 (Nakano 2024, Beddok 2025);
(b) No approved preventive pharmacology exists in any CIN/genomic-instability disorder;
(c) Two candidate archetypes live: mechanism-matched approved candidates (everolimus-TSC, lonafarnib-HGPS),
and long-standing-retail-borrow candidates (metformin-LFS). The readthrough/ASO/stabilization
candidates elsewhere in the package should be mapped onto axis-specific precedent models.
- **Should NOT claim**: (a) That any CIN syndrome has an approved preventive drug (false);
(b) That metformin is precedent for pediatric CIN (it is not - metformin-LFS is adult-only);
(c) That celecoxib or any NSAID/Clebrex-style drug is a currently-approved precedent (withdrawn);
(d) That "cGAS-STING" = approved therapeutic pathway - JAKinibs are off-label. 

## Key GAPS (open, explicit)

1. **No pediatric trial data for any preventive pharmacology in any constitutional cancer-risk syndrome** other than everolimus-TSC - MILI metformin-LFS and other chemoprevention protocols are adult-only.
2. **Metformin-HGPS** - only Medical Hypotheses-level papers found (Finley 2014, Finley 2015, PMIDs 25216752/26115946); no preclinical or clinical evidence.
3. **No drug approved today for prevention in ANY constitutional cancer-risk disorder** - the only past example (celecoxib-FAP) was withdrawn.
4. **No pediatric-legal mechanism-based drug alternative to everolimus-TSC identified** for any other CIN/genomic-instability disorder - this is the class's drug-precise-ceiling.
5. HGPS combination evidence was found for lonafarnib+pravastatin+zoledronate only (Gordon 2014 Circulation); **no HGPS lonafarnib-rapalog or lonafarnib-metformin data located** - assert as GAP rather than infer a "metformin add-on trial exists today."
