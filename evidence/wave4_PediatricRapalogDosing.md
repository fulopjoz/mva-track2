# Wave 4 - Q9 PediatricRapalogDosing

**Question:** Can the report name a concrete, monitorable pediatric exposure window for rapalogs at
age ~5 - with TSC-EXIST trough practice, transplant experience at 5 years, BSA-scaled PK, and
long-course monitoring standards - and can the adult-derived Mannick immunoenhancing window be
translated to pediatrics?

**Author wave:** wave4, 2026-09-05. One gap (P2-7, "no pediatric dose anchor" for the Mannick
floor) is what this axis was dispatched against.

---

## VERDICT (first)

**YES - a concrete, monitorable pediatric exposure window can be named, tiered in three rungs,
each with direct documentation at or around age 5.** The pediatric-anchor gap is narrowed to
explicit, bounded missing numbers rather than "unmeasured":

1. **On-label therapeutic band (documented ≥1 y):** everolimus, BSA-dosed
   (4.5 mg/m²/day SEGA; 5 mg/m²/day TSC-seizures - ≈ 3.4 / 3.8 mg/day at age 5, 18 kg, 0.75 m²),
   titrated by LC-MS/MS whole-blood trough to **5–15 ng/mL**, with trough checks 1–2 weeks after
   start/dose change, every 3–6 months while BSA is changing, 6–12 months at stable BSA
   (FDA Afinitor/Afinitor Disperz label, Table 1 TDM scheme; indications ≥1 y SEGA, ≥2 y seizures).
2. **Lowest pediatric-validated efficacy floor: 3 ng/mL.** EXIST-3's low-trough arm
   (3–7 ng/mL) was efficacious in children from age 2.2 - the pediatric evidence floor is
   therefore 3 ng/mL, not 5. A trough floor at 3–5 ng/mL is a nameable, trial-validated,
   monitorable low band.
3. **Mannick-class sub-band (<3 ng/mL): NOT pediatric-validated.** The Mannick regimen
   (everolimus 0.1–0.5 mg/day, 6 weeks, elderly ≈65+) is arithmetically ~7–40× below a
   5-year-old's label exposure → predicted trough ≪ 3 ng/mL. No pediatric trial at that exposure
   exists (recorded negative, 2 queries below). The immunoenhancement *direction* remains
   adult-only. **However**, the translatability gap is substantially narrowed by new evidence
   this wave located: children (mean age 4.7 y) on sirolimus at troughs 3.5–10 ng/mL retain
   vaccine immunogenicity - including **live** MMR - with normal lymphocyte subsets
   (Yuan 2025/2026, KHE cohorts, n=56+16). The Mannick floor can therefore be restated as
   "low end of the pediatric-validated band (3–5 ng/mL), where chronic tolerance and
   humoral/vaccine immunity are directly documented in 5-year-olds" rather than as an
   unanchored extrapolation.

**Documented age-5 continuous multi-year exposure - YES, multiple independent lines:**
- EXIST-1 final: median exposure 47.1 months (max 58.3), 83.8 % of patients <18 y; emergent-AE
  incidence decreased over time; growth SDS and puberty not adversely affected (Franz 2016).
- EXIST-1 phase-1/2 5-year analysis: median exposure 67.8 months (4.7–83.2), patients ≥3 y, zero
  AE discontinuations, mostly grade 1/2 AEs (Franz 2015). A child starting at 3 is on drug through
  age ~8–10 in these data.
- EXIST-3 (≥2.2 y; 28 % <6 y) with trough arms 3–7/9–15 ng/mL; 2-year extension RR 57.7 %;
  post-extension trough target 5–15, sustained response (French 2016/2018; Franz 2021). **Honesty
  point: two pediatric treatment-related infection deaths (pneumonia, septic shock) are on the
  EXIST-3 record** - the AE clause of any prevention-context statement must carry this.
- Kotulska 2013: 8 children <3 y on everolimus, mean follow-up 35 months - i.e., continuous
  exposure crossing age 5 - AE incidence similar to older children.
- Saffari 2019: 17 TSC children started on everolimus at median 5 months; no grade 3–4 AEs;
  treatment continued into ages 0–6 cohort range.
- PREVENT (Capal 2024): phase 1 **prophylactic** sirolimus in asymptomatic TSC infants <6 months
  treated to 12 months: 94 % of troughs in the 5–15 ng/mL target, only 2 grade-3 AEs (lipids),
  all resolved by 24 months - the feasibility datapoint for dosing a child with *no clinical
  indication*, i.e., the true prevention analog.
- Transplant: prospective 3-year everolimus in de novo pediatric renal transplant
  (children <16, 8 in extension <10 y; Ettenger 2008); sirolimus in pediatric cardiac recipients
  from age 6 months, BSA-dosed 7 mg/m² to trough 5–15 µg/L (Lobach 2005); sirolimus phase-1 PK
  in children **5–11 y** (Tejani 2004).

**AE profile at age ~5 (named, not generic):** the FDA label's age-stratified data - <6 y vs ≥6 y
 -  exist and are the correct citation set for a 5-year-old: EXIST-1: ≥1 infection 96 % vs 67 %,
serious infection 35 % vs 7 %; EXIST-3: 77 % vs 53 %, serious 16 % vs 4 %. Grade 3/4 AE ~40 %
across the ≥2 y EXIST-3 extension population. Label-imposed pediatric operational standards:
complete the **childhood live-vaccine series before starting** (accelerated schedule permitted),
PJP prophylaxis when combined with other immunosuppressants, CBC q6mo/annual, renal function,
fasting glucose, lipids baseline + periodic.

**What remains missed (explicit missing data, for the report's own gap sentence):**
- No pediatric NK-pool or NK-function measurement under rapalogs at any trough (extends wave-3's
  recorded gap; new vaccine/humoral data do not cover NK).
- No rapalog dosed in children below trough 3 ng/mL in any located trial.
- No pharmacokinetic dataset at Mannick-class doses in children.
- Age-5-stratified mortality/late-effects registry table beyond trial publications (TOSCA PASS is
  real-world and long-course but not age-5 stratified).

at age 0.8 (SEGA) and 2.2 (seizures). Confidence: **high**.

gap is narrowed to: no NK-specific data at any trough; no data approaching the Mannick exposure.

## Consequences for the MVA report (controller-facing)

1. The "floored at the adult-derived Mannick window" clause (attack map senolysis rule 2;
   P2-7) should be reworded to: *"floored at trough 3–5 ng/mL - the lowest pediatric
   efficacy-validated band (EXIST-3 LT, ≥2.2 y) - with the adult Mannick immunoenhancement datum
   cited as direction-setting only; the Mannick exposure itself (≈0.1–0.5 mg/day adult
   equivalents) is out of any pediatric evidence and must not be targeted."*
2. A concrete monitorable window IS nameable: **start 4.5–5 mg/m²/day, titrate to trough
   5–10 ng/mL (lower half of label band), TDM per FDA label calendar (1–2 wk post-change;
   q3–6 mo while growing), CBC/renal/glucose/lipids per label, complete live vaccines before
   start; dose-hold borrowings per NK rule set.** Duration precedent to cite: 47.1-mo median
   EXIST-1 final / 67.8-mo median ph1/2 / infancy-through-age-6 cohorts.
3. The pediatric infection-safety honesty clause must include the two EXIST-3 pediatric
   treatment-related infection deaths + the label <6-y stratified infection ratios.
4. PREVENT (Capal 2024) answers the "prophylactic rapalog in an asymptomatic child with a
   constitutional mTORopathy is feasible and well tolerated" sentence - the only true prevention
   analog located; grade C, n=5, so cited as feasibility, not efficacy.
